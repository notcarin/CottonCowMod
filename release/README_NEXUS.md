COTTON COW MOD - 1.0.2
Adds two player-owned cows as late-game livestock, as well as new relationship levels with Farmer Cotton and Young Tom Cotton.

:: REQUIREMENTS ::

* Tales of the Shire (up to date)
* BepInEx 5 (x64)
* Tales of the Shire Modding Tools

:: FEATURES ::

* Two unlockable cows tied to Cotton family friendship, living in your glade
* Extended relationship levels (11-13) for Farmer Cotton and Young Tom Cotton
* New level-up rewards at each added level
* Placeable Cow Feeding Trough (appears in Garden Storage)
* Daily feeding system — accepts vegetables, grains, and apples
* Milk production with quality based on feeding streak
* Letters from Cotton NPCs when cows are unlocked
* Teaser letters if you've earned a cow but haven't unlocked the glade yet
* Built-in bug report button (Settings → Mods)

:: HOW IT WORKS ::
Build your friendship with Farmer Cotton and Young Tom Cotton beyond the base game's level cap. At level 13 each will gift you a cow — complete with a letter and a Cow Feeding Trough delivered to your Garden Storage.

Your cows live in the glade: the last garden expansion, unlocked at Gardening Club level 5. If you reach level 13 before that, you'll get a letter saying you need more room; your cow arrives the morning after you unlock the glade. You don't need to clear or move anything in it.

Place the Cow Feeding Trough (not the game's Metal Feeding Trough, which looks the same) and fill it daily. Feed your cow consistently and she'll produce milk each morning. The longer your feeding streak, the better the quality. Miss a day and your streak resets. A hungry cow will follow you around the glade until you fill the trough.

If your backpack is full when you collect milk, it's sent to your mailbox instead.

:: REPORTING A BUG ::

Go to Settings → Mods and turn on "Create Bug Report" under Cotton Cow Mod. A report is copied to your clipboard (just paste it into your comment or bug report) and also saved to your Desktop as CottonCowMod_BugReport.txt. It contains the mod's view of your save and recent log lines, with your Windows user name removed.

:: INSTALLATION ::

1. Install BepInEx 5 (x64) — extract into your game folder
2. Install Tales of the Shire Modding Tools — extract into your game folder
3. Place CottonCowMod.dll in BepInEx/plugins/CottonCowMod/
4. Launch the game

See the included INSTALL\_GUIDE for detailed step-by-step instructions.

:: COMPATIBILITY ::

* Your cows' milk, feeding streak and arrival are saved in each save slot, so every save has its own cows
* Uninstall by deleting the BepInEx/plugins/CottonCowMod/ folder. Your save stays playable: without the mod, the Cottons show as level 10 and their shops work normally, and the Cow Feeding Trough disappears. Reinstall and your cows and the Cottons' real levels come back, with a new trough in your Storage
* Upgrading from 1.0.1 or earlier: if you uninstalled an older version and the Cottons' shops went empty, install 1.0.2, load that save and sleep once. The save is fixed, and you can uninstall again afterwards
* Using WeMod or other memory injectors alongside BepInEx mods may cause occasional crashes

:: KNOWN ISSUES ::

* No custom cow model — uses existing game assets
* No additional recipes using cow milk (yet)
* Letters and the trough's text are English only
